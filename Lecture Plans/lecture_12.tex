\textbf{Example 4.3.2} \par

First the dot product
\begin{align*}
\vect{a}\cdot\vect{b} = (2\vect{i}-4\vect{k})\cdot(2\vect{i}-2\sqrt{2}\vect{j}-2\sqrt{2}\vect{k}) = 4(1 + 2\sqrt{2})
\end{align*}

Find the magnitudes
\begin{align*}
|\vect{a}| &= \sqrt{4+16} = \sqrt{20}\\
|\vect{b}| &= \sqrt{4 + 8 + 8} = \sqrt{20}
\end{align*}

So
\begin{align*}
    \cos \theta = 1/5(1 + 2\sqrt{2}) \approx 0.7 \; {\rm rad}
\end{align*}

    \vect{i} & \vect{j} & \vect{k}\\
    2 & -1 & 3\\
    3 & 2 & -4

\textbf{Example 4.4.3} \par

First take the determinant from the previous question
\begin{align*}
\vect{a}\times\vect{b} = \begin{vmatrix}
    \vect{i} & \vect{j} & \vect{k}\\
    2 & -1 & 3\\
    3 & 2 & -4
\end{vmatrix} &= -2\vect{i} + 17\vect{j} + 7\vect{k}
\end{align*}
Now let's try multiplying different rows of it by $\alpha = 5$
\begin{align*}
(5\vect{a})\times\vect{b} = \begin{vmatrix}
    \vect{i} & \vect{j} & \vect{k}\\
    5\times2 & 5\times(-1) & 5\times3\\
    3 & 2 & -4
\end{vmatrix} &= 5\times(-2)\vect{i} + 5\times17\vect{j} + 5\times7\vect{k} \\
&= 5 (\vect{a}\times\vect{b}) 
\end{align*}
Useful note $(5\vect{a})\times (5\vect{b}) = 25(\vect{a}\times\vect{b})$.
